Magic Melodies 🎹

A piano tiles game for little kids, for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no reading, no game over.

Contact

Questions, a bug, or a song request? Email jj@joeljenkins.me. Please mention your device and iOS version if something isn't working.

How do I open the settings?

Press and hold the ⚙️ in the top-right corner of the home screen for about a second and a half, until the ring fills. That keeps little fingers out of the settings.

How do I unlock all the songs?

Five songs are free. To unlock the rest, press and hold the 🎁 card on the home screen for 3 seconds (or open the settings and tap "Unlock all songs"), then answer the question for grown-ups. It's a one-time purchase with no subscription, and Apple handles the payment.

I already bought it. How do I get the songs back?

On a new device or after reinstalling, open the settings and tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. If Family Sharing's Ask to Buy is on, the songs unlock as soon as the request is approved.

There's no sound

Turn up the volume with the side buttons. The app plays sound even when the silent switch is on. If you just changed the instrument in the settings, give it a second to load.

How do I keep my child in the app?

Turn on Guided Access in the iOS Settings app (Accessibility → Guided Access). Then triple-click the side or top button while Magic Melodies is open to lock the device to it.
