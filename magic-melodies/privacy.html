Magic Melodies Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Magic Melodies is a music game for young children. It is built to collect nothing.

No data collected

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no analytics.

No network use

The app works offline and does not connect to any server of its own. The only exception is Apple's App Store: iOS contacts it to show the price of the one-time unlock, and to complete or restore that purchase.

No ads. One optional purchase, handled by Apple

There are no advertisements and no links out of the app. Five songs are free, and a single optional in-app purchase unlocks the rest. Apple handles that purchase: the app never sees your name, Apple Account, or payment details, and only learns whether the unlock was bought. The purchase screen sits behind a question for grown-ups, so children can't reach it.

Settings on the device

Your choices, such as speed, number of lanes, and instrument, and whether all songs are unlocked, are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. They never leave the device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
